It’s crazy how tiny and interconnected the elite academic world was 50+ years ago. In the videos linked in the quoted post, Freeman Dyson mentions talking about science with his close childhood friend, Christopher Longuet-Higgins.

Dyson was part of a group of four tremendously precocious boys studying together at Winchester.

The group also included Christopher’s brother, Michael, as well as James Lighthill, famous in AI circles today for his Lighthill Report, which is credited with causing the first AI Winter.

Reading Longuet-Higgins’ profile on Wikipedia just now, I was reminded that he was the doctoral advisor to none other than Geoff Hinton! 

So he was closely connected to both the man who killed AI and the man who arguably did the most to keep the dream alive during its long period of dormancy.

He was also the doctoral advisor to Peter Higgs (of Higgs boson fame), which isn’t too shabby, either! 

Lot of alpha on your surname beginning with “Higg,” apparently…
