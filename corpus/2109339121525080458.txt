My 5-year-old daughter Roz and her sister Iris (age 3) were playing with Audacity on some ancient Windows laptop I set up for them so they could “work” alongside daddy in my home office, and they had an amusing conversation about possible names for future siblings.

But then Roz got upset because she lost her place while reviewing the audio file in Audacity and was despondent about not being able to find a particular location in the file. 

Luckily, I have FrankenWhisper and Claude to transcribe and review the footage, so I was able to tell her to scrub to 2:06 in the audio file to find the place she wanted. See pic for details.
