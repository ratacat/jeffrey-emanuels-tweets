Quick update on this system now that I've expanded it to go all the way back to 2005 for both holdings data and for my style factor decomposition of returns (similar to Barra style factors). 

I asked it first of all to give me the best stock pickers over the last 5 years, 10 years, and since 2005, where "best" is based on what I call the "Alpha Adjusted HolderIQ."

This tracks a simulated portfolio based on each 13-F filer and what would be knowable to an outsider at the time if they wanted to try to replicate the portfolio. Obviously, this doesn't include shorts, many international stocks, positions held via swap, funky securities, etc. 

It's also not relevant for HFT firms like RenTec; knowing their holdings on one day doesn't necessarily tell you much about their holdings a week later. Whereas for a fund like Pershing Square, it tells you a whole lot, since they run a concentrated book that skews long, and they don't trade in and out frequently.

But all those limitations notwithstanding, this kind of analysis can still give some very interesting results that can be useful in many ways.
