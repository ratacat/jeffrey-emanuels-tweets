It would’ve been nice to have been included in the Astra early access list. I’m always a bit confused about how that works, since you’d think by any sensible criteria I’d be included. 

But I actually think it’s better for me that I not get special treatment. I pay for my tokens like anyone else and get the models when everyone does. Why? Isn’t this pure cope?

My reasoning, which I realized today while bemoaning my lack of Astra, is that otherwise people would try to dismiss my work by saying something like “Well, if *I* got early access and unlimited tokens, too, then I could do all that stuff.”

I have the same tools as other people and no special access. Anyone could theoretically do what I’m doing on a smaller scale with just a couple of accounts. It does require a lot of time, effort, and dedication, but it doesn’t require being part of some special club

My goal all along has been to show how anyone can do this stuff. That’s why I’m so open with my workflows, prompts, and why I give away all my tools and libraries as free, open-source software.

There’s an old joke, recounted by Freeman Dyson but originally by Oppenheimer, about how their colleague Julian Schwinger used to present physics to an audience:

“When ordinary people give a talk, it is to tell you how to do it… but when Julian Schwinger gives a talk, it’s to demonstrate that only Julian Schwinger could do it!”

As much as Schwinger’s colleagues admired his brains and algebraic mastery, they would have respected him more if he had acted less like a magician and more like a teacher. There’s a good lesson in that for all of us.
