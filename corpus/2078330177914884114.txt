People joke about Anthropic’s repeated backpedaling over Fable leaving the Max plan (I myself have made a few posts in jest about it), but it’s actually pretty genius marketing. 

It has now been reinforced multiple times that Fable was this valuable rare bird that was a special treat: too expensive under normal circumstances, but you were getting a limited complimentary sample just so you could appreciate how great it is.

And that halo persists psychologically even after the announcement tonight that Fable will be a permanent part of the Max plan going forward.

It just feels different to use Fable now than it does Sol. Like you’re getting away with something, being allowed to walk past the velvet rope. 

If they had made it a regular part of the Max plan from the beginning, like OpenAI did with Sol and the GPT Pro plan, it wouldn’t feel that way.
