I was inspired by that spatial source code browser demo by Rik Arends making the rounds to make something similar, just for personal use and to see how fast I could make it using Apple Silicon and Metal. 

Here's what I have so far, showing the files in my Asupersync project, which is over 5mm lines of code, on a 6K screen.

You can install it on Mac with "brew install --cask dicklesworthstone/tap/franken-code-browser" (it should also be on the Mac App Store soon), and it's also open-source:
