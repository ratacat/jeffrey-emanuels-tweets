It’s sad to me because I’ve always been a huge reader ever since I was a little kid and all throughout my 30s. 

But in the last year I’ve read far fewer full books, mostly because I find it more stimulating to work actively or to engage in more interactive learning with AI. 

The books I do “read” now are mostly audio books that I listen to when my eyes are just too tired from working 16 hours a day. But I retain those less well.

At least I still have all the knowledge that I gained from reading all those hundreds of long books in the past. But I think it’s pretty hopeless to expect that from the vast majority of young people today.
